The people behind ParkSmart

About us.

We are an eight-member student team at Asia Pacific University, exploring how a clearer view of campus parking could make getting around easier.

Our project

Less circling. A smarter campus.

APU ParkSmart is a student-built website concept for campus parking. It brings parking availability, account and vehicle registration, and parking-session information into one simple experience.

Our idea supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 9: Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure by exploring a digital approach to campus services.
