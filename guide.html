The quick start

How to use ParkSmart

01↗

Set up your account

Create an account once and save your first car. Add other cars from your Home profile.

02▤

Start and end parking

Choose a saved car on Register Parking. Start when you arrive and end from Home when you leave.

03⌖

Know your rate

Visitors: RM 1/hour, no grace period. Students and Lecturer / Staff: RM 5/day, with 10 minutes to end without a charge.

Demo note Availability is simulated. Zone C’s “Visitors only” label is informational, and no real payment is processed.

Good to know

Quick answers

Is parking availability live?

No. The dashboard uses sample figures; it is not connected to parking sensors.

How will I know where my car is parked?

Exact location is not available because sensors are not connected in this demo.

Can I pay in the demo?

No. The debit-card option only shows a simulated result.
