The quick start

How to use ParkSmart

01↗

Register for a space

  1. Create your account once with your name, email, password, account type, first vehicle plate, and car colour. Students use a TP number (TP + 6 digits); Lecturer / Staff enter a staff ID.
  2. Sign in on a later visit. Add extra vehicle plates from the Profile panel on Home.
  3. Open Register Parking and choose one of your saved vehicles. There is no zone or duration to select.
  4. Visitors pay RM 1.00 per hour from the start, with no grace period. Students and Lecturer / Staff pay RM 5.00 per Malaysia calendar day and have 10 minutes to end parking without a charge.
  5. Start parking when you arrive. Home shows your car, time parked, and current price.
  6. End parking when you leave. The final demo charge is calculated from the actual parked time; no real payment is processed.
  7. Home shows the car’s zone and space number when connected parking sensors report its location. Sensors are not connected in this demo.
02▤

Confirm your demo booking

  1. Choose a registered vehicle and start parking. No payment is taken at the start.
  2. End your session on Home when you leave. Visitors are charged RM 1.00 per hour; students and Lecturer / Staff are charged RM 5.00 per Malaysia calendar day after the 10-minute grace period.
  3. This is a demo: no actual payment is collected.

Stay safe. Never share your card PIN. ParkSmart’s demo will not ask for it.

03⌖

Find an available spot

  1. Scan the Instagram QR code in the dashboard footer to open the APU ParkSmart Instagram page.
  2. Check the live dashboard for total spaces and each zone’s status.
  3. Available means more than 30% of spaces are free; Nearly Full means 1–30% are free; Full means no spaces are free.
  4. Check “Last updated” so you know how recent the count is.

Demo note. Slot counts and the 3D bay lights change in your browser. Parking sensors are not connected, so a car’s exact location is not available yet.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is this a live APU parking service?

This is a student project demo. Slot counts change to demonstrate the dashboard; no real parking is reserved and no real payment or refund is processed.

How much does parking cost in this demo?

Visitors pay RM 1.00 per hour from the start, with no grace period. Students and Lecturer / Staff pay RM 5.00 per Malaysia calendar day and can end parking within 10 minutes without a charge.

How will I know where my car is parked?

Connected parking sensors can report the car’s zone and space number to Home. Sensors are not connected in this demo, so the exact location is unavailable.

Does ParkSmart process payments?

No. The prototype calculates a demo parking charge but does not request card details or process a real transaction.

How are my TP number and vehicle plate used?

Account details and saved vehicle plates are stored in the configured Supabase project. Do not use sensitive personal information in a demo deployment.
