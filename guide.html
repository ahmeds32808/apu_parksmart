The quick start

How to use ParkSmart

01↗

Register for a space

  1. Enter your student TP number (TP + 6 digits).
  2. Add your vehicle plate number.
  3. Choose a parking zone and your duration.
  4. Check the amount and available slots.
  5. Tap Register & Pay to continue.
02▤

Pay with a debit card

  1. Enter the card number, name on card, expiry date, and CVV in the payment window.
  2. Review your booking summary before confirming.
  3. See your booking reference and head to your selected zone.

Stay safe. Never share your card PIN. ParkSmart’s demo will not ask for it.

03⌖

Find an available spot

  1. Check the live dashboard for total spaces and each zone’s status.
  2. Green means plenty of spaces; amber means filling up; red means almost full.
  3. Check “Last updated” so you know how recent the count is.
  4. Head to a zone with green status and park in an open bay.

Where does the data come from? Indoor IoT bay sensors and outdoor computer-vision cameras report slot changes to the dashboard.

No space? No charge.

10-Minute Grace Period

If you enter but can’t find a space, leave within 10 minutes and the gate’s Automated License Plate Recognition (ALPR) system automatically waives your entry charge.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is this a live APU parking service?

This is a student project demo. Slot counts change to demonstrate the live dashboard; no real parking is reserved and no real payment is taken.

How much does parking cost in this demo?

The demo rate is RM 1.00 per hour, for up to 12 hours. The amount updates automatically when you change your duration.

What happens if my selected zone is full?

You can’t submit a registration for a zone with no available slots. Check the live dashboard and choose a zone with spaces available.

Does the payment demo store my card details?

No. The demo does not send or store payment details and does not process a real transaction. Do not enter real card details.
